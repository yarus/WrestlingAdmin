# Grill a plan

Inspect the plan and relevant repository evidence first. If the plan is missing and cannot be inferred from context, ask what to examine.

Ask one question at a time, working through dependencies before downstream decisions. Focus on consequential assumptions, tradeoffs, constraints, and failure modes. Include a recommended answer and its reason with each question; distinguish evidence from assumptions. Answer questions the codebase can resolve by inspecting it instead of asking the user.

Use an available user-input tool when appropriate, or ask directly if none is available. Keep the interview within the requested scope, adapt to answers, and stop when the user asks or the material decisions are resolved. Finish with agreed decisions and remaining uncertainties. An interview alone does not authorize implementing the plan.
